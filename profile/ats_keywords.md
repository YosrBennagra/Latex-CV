# ATS Keyword Bank — per Target Role

> Keywords an ATS is likely to scan for, mapped to what the profile can honestly
> claim (all items verified against `technologies.md`). When tailoring a CV:
> 1. Read the job posting and highlight its keywords.
> 2. Keep only keywords that appear BOTH in the posting AND in this bank.
> 3. Use the exact spelling the posting uses (e.g. "React.js" vs "React").
> 4. Put the most important keywords in the Skills section AND once in an
>    experience bullet — ATS ranks repeated-in-context keywords higher.
